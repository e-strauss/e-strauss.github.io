Teaching assistant for MLMMI (course website by the instructor, Arnab Phani), an advanced course
on the system and data management challenges of managing and serving machine learning models.

The course goes beyond standard training pipelines and covers model management practices such as
versioning, lineage tracking, and monitoring; modern serving architectures for both traditional
ML models and large language models; and emerging topics such as ML agents and inference
optimization techniques.
